Our hiring process

Every engineering candidate goes through the same stages:

  1. Recruiter call (30 min): motivation, logistics.
  2. Take-home exercise: a small payments API, about 3 hours. We pay for your time.
  3. Take-home review: walk us through your solution and extend it live.
  4. System design interview (60 min): design a part of a payments platform.
  5. Values interview: behavioural questions about ownership and written communication.

Tip: we care more about clear trade-offs than perfect code. Write a short README with your take-home.
